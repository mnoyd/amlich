## Decisions Made

- **Year Basis**: Solar year (Gregorian calendar) - input year used directly without lunar conversion
- **Kua 5 Resolution**: Male → 8, Female → 2 (frozen project policy)
- **Gender Encoding**: Enum with Male and Female variants for type safety
- **East/West Group**: Odd Kua numbers (1,3,4,9) = East, Even Kua numbers (2,6,7,8) = West
- **Direction Sets**:
  - East favorable: North, South, East, Southeast
  - East unfavorable: Northwest, Southwest, West, Northeast
  - West favorable: Northwest, Southwest, West, Northeast
  - West unfavorable: North, South, East, Southeast
- **Formula**: Sum year digits to single digit, then:
  - 1900-1999 male: subtract from 10
  - 1900-1999 female: add 5
  - 2000+ male: subtract from 9
  - 2000+ female: add 5
  - Apply Kua 5 resolution if result is 5
