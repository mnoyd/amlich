# Lunar Health / Seasonal Wellness Research

**Domain:** Tier-0 historical branch–channel associations and seasonal routine guidance
**Researched:** 2026-08-11
**Confidence:** HIGH on the source distinction and fixed tables; MEDIUM on product wording; LOW on any clinical interpretation
**Candidate milestone:** P3, narrowed to non-clinical cultural information

> **Recommendation:** Do not name a Tier-0 fixed two-hour lookup “Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú.” In the cited classical material, `子午流注` is a substantially more complex acupuncture-point opening method based on day and hour stems/branches. A fixed Earthly-Branch → channel lookup is separately titled `十二經納地支` (Twelve Channels Assigned to the Earthly Branches). Ship only the latter plus four-season routine excerpts, if this milestone proceeds. Defer all point selection and clinical advice.

This note labels claims as either:

- **[SOURCE]** — stated directly by a classical text, facsimile/transcription, or official institution.
- **[DESIGN]** — an Amlich product or engineering inference. These statements must not be presented as classical or medical facts.

## Executive decision

### Safe enough to freeze now

1. **A historical twelve-branch → twelve-channel association table**, named `TwelveBranchChannelAssociation` / *Thập nhị kinh nạp địa chi*, not “organ activity” and not full Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú.
2. **Four seasonal routine profiles** faithfully paraphrased from *Huangdi Neijing Suwen*, chapter `四氣調神大論` (*Great Discourse on Regulating the Spirit According to the Four Seasons*).
3. **A 24-term → four-season grouping** whose boundaries are Lập Xuân, Lập Hạ, Lập Thu, and Lập Đông. The term sequence and the “four beginnings” are source-grounded; applying one of four *Suwen* profiles to all six terms in a season is explicitly an Amlich design mapping.
4. **A mandatory cultural-information envelope** stating that the output describes a historical tradition, does not establish scientific validity or clinical efficacy, and is not diagnosis, prevention, or treatment guidance.

### Must be deferred

- Full `子午流注` / Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú (`納甲法`) calculation.
- Any “open point,” acupuncture point, needle, moxibustion, acupressure, treatment-time, dosage, or procedure output.
- Claims that a channel or organ is “active,” “at peak,” “detoxing,” “weak,” “blocked,” or diagnostically meaningful at a given hour.
- Disease, symptom, risk, prevention, cure, mitigation, or treatment claims.
- Food, herb, supplement, fasting, or quantified sleep/exercise prescriptions by solar term.
- Tier-2 Bazi personalization. None of the sources reviewed establishes a Bazi → medical recommendation rule.
- Twenty-four unique health prescriptions. The cited *Suwen* chapter supplies four seasonal profiles, not a separate regimen for each solar term.
- Any statement that WHO classification or standardization endorses the scientific validity or efficacy of the tradition.

## 1. Load-bearing source distinction

### 1.1 What classical `子午流注` actually contains

**[SOURCE]** *Zhenjiu Dacheng* (*Châm Cứu Đại Thành*), volume 5, contains the `徐氏子午流注逐日按時定穴歌` (Xu-style song for selecting points by day and hour), day/hour tables, assignments of channels to the ten Heavenly Stems, five-shu points, original points, generating/combining rules, and rules for points being open or closed. Its examples change the selected channel and point according to both the day's stem and the hour's stem/branch; for example, the Jiǎ-day sequence begins at the Gallbladder well point at Jiǎ-Xū hour and proceeds through other channels and point classes. The text's own explanation defines `子午流注` in terms of yin-yang pairing, cyclical qi/blood, and timed opening/closing of points. See the [primary text transcription with linked page images, volume 5](https://www.shidianguji.com/book/NA08718/chapter/1l0gx6qnyiz2h), especially the sections `徐氏子午流注逐日按時定穴歌`, `流注圖`, and `論子午流注法`; a [Chinese Text Project facsimile record](https://ctext.org/library.pl?if=en&res=2772) identifies the scanned work as Yang Jizhou's *Zhenjiu Dacheng*.

**[SOURCE]** A 2025 World Federation of Chinese Medicine Societies training notice describes the `納甲法` as a ten-day timed structure combining the twelve regular channels, ten Heavenly Stems, five-shu points, five-phase generation, and original points. It traces the method through Yan Mingguang and Xu Feng into *Zhenjiu Dacheng*. This contemporary first-party description agrees with the structure visible in the classical text. See the [WFCMS course description](https://wfcms.org/show/11/7609.html).

**[DESIGN]** Therefore, a function that only maps the current two-hour Earthly-Branch slot to one channel is not an implementation of full `子午流注`. Calling it that would overstate both scope and source fidelity.

### 1.2 The simple fixed cycle has a different title

**[SOURCE]** *Zhenjiu Daquan*, volume 5, separately labels the fixed mnemonic `十二經納地支歌` (Song of the Twelve Channels Assigned to the Earthly Branches), immediately beside—but distinct from—the Heavenly-Stem song and the point-opening material. Its table is: [Chinese Text Project, `卷之五 論子午流注之法`, lines 3–9](https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=688012&if=en).

| Earthly Branch | Civil-time display convention | Historically associated channel |
|---|---:|---|
| Tý / 子 | 23:00–01:00 | Gallbladder / Đởm / 足少陽膽 |
| Sửu / 丑 | 01:00–03:00 | Liver / Can / 足厥陰肝 |
| Dần / 寅 | 03:00–05:00 | Lung / Phế / 手太陰肺 |
| Mão / 卯 | 05:00–07:00 | Large Intestine / Đại trường / 手陽明大腸 |
| Thìn / 辰 | 07:00–09:00 | Stomach / Vị / 足陽明胃 |
| Tỵ / 巳 | 09:00–11:00 | Spleen / Tỳ / 足太陰脾 |
| Ngọ / 午 | 11:00–13:00 | Heart / Tâm / 手少陰心 |
| Mùi / 未 | 13:00–15:00 | Small Intestine / Tiểu trường / 手太陽小腸 |
| Thân / 申 | 15:00–17:00 | Bladder / Bàng quang / 足太陽膀胱 |
| Dậu / 酉 | 17:00–19:00 | Kidney / Thận / 足少陰腎 |
| Tuất / 戌 | 19:00–21:00 | Pericardium / Tâm bào / 手厥陰心包 |
| Hợi / 亥 | 21:00–23:00 | Triple Burner / Tam tiêu / 手少陽三焦 |

**[SOURCE]** *Zhenjiu Dacheng* also prints the circulation sequence from Lung through the twelve channels and assigns the branch hours in that order; its channel sections include statements such as the Lung channel receiving at Dần hour. See the [volume 7 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E9%87%9D%E7%81%B8%E5%A4%A7%E6%88%90/%E5%8D%B7%E4%B8%83).

**[DESIGN]** The civil-time ranges above reuse Amlich's existing hour-branch contract (`Tý = 23:00–01:00`). The classical verses name double-hours but do not specify modern time zones, daylight-saving rules, longitude correction, or “true solar time.” Store `time_basis: "local_civil_hour_branch"` in evidence rather than attributing that convention to the classical source.

### 1.3 The fixed cycle is historically contested

**[SOURCE]** *Zhenjiu Fengyuan* (*Acupuncture Source*), volume 4, records the same Lung-at-Dần fixed allocation and then criticizes treating the twelve channels as one two-hour block each: its author argues that the classical circulation is fifty circuits per day and asks how Large Intestine could simply belong to Mão hour. See [Chinese Text Project, `卷四 經穴考正`](https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=414318&if=en).

**[SOURCE]** *Maijue Huibian*, volume 10, likewise preserves the mnemonic but objects that channels differ greatly in length and number of points and therefore cannot simply be distributed one per time block; it records the verse while explicitly awaiting correction. See [the primary-text transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E8%84%88%E8%A8%A3%E5%8C%AF%E8%BE%A8), section `十二經流注時序歌`.

**[DESIGN]** The API must expose this as a historical association, not a physiological fact. Record the criticism as a `KnownDivergence`, not as an error to “resolve” by picking whichever modern organ-clock chart is most common.

## 2. Seasonal source boundary

### 2.1 What *Suwen* directly supports

**[SOURCE]** `四氣調神大論` gives one profile for each of four three-month seasons, not twenty-four solar-term-specific prescriptions. In concise paraphrase:

| Season | Source-grounded routine themes |
|---|---|
| Spring | Rise early; take an unhurried outdoor walk; loosen the body; let intention and activity unfold rather than constrain them. |
| Summer | Rise early; engage with daylight; avoid anger; allow activity and attention to move outward. |
| Autumn | Sleep and rise early; settle attention; gather it inward and keep it tranquil. |
| Winter | Sleep early and rise later, waiting for daylight; keep intention inward; seek warmth and avoid unnecessary exposure to cold. |

The Chinese text and a linked facsimile base are available at [Chinese Text Project, `黃帝內經 · 素問 · 四氣調神大論`](https://ctext.org/huangdi-neijing/si-qi-diao-shen-da-lun/zh) and the [Sibu Congkan facsimile record](https://ctext.org/library.pl?if=en&res=77415).

**[SOURCE]** The chapter also attaches organ injury and later illness consequences to acting contrary to each season. Those are claims inside the historical text; they are not needed for an informational routine surface and are not evidence of modern clinical validity.

**[DESIGN]** Omit all organ-injury and disease-consequence clauses from product advice. Preserve them only, if needed, inside provenance notes identifying what was deliberately not surfaced.

### 2.2 How 24 solar terms may be joined to four profiles

**[SOURCE]** The China Meteorological Administration lists the 24 terms in canonical order and states that Lập Xuân, Lập Hạ, Lập Thu, and Lập Đông mark seasonal beginnings. It also warns that the terms originated in Yellow River phenology and that actual seasonal weather varies greatly by region. See [CMA, origin and names of the 24 solar terms](https://www.cma.gov.cn/2011xzt/20150130/2015020302/201502/t20150202_273618.html) and [CMA, term categories and the four seasonal beginnings](https://www.cma.gov.cn/2011xzt/essjqzt/jqhz/jqhz02/201312/t20131213_233949.html).

**[SOURCE]** UNESCO describes the 24 terms as divisions of the sun's annual motion used traditionally as a framework for production and daily routines; that inscription documents cultural heritage, not medical efficacy. See the [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage entry](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/the-twenty-four-solar-terms-knowledge-in-china-of-time-and-practices-developed-through-observation-of-the-suns-annual-motion-00647).

**[DESIGN]** Freeze this grouping:

| Seasonal profile | Solar terms |
|---|---|
| Spring | Lập Xuân, Vũ Thủy, Kinh Trập, Xuân Phân, Thanh Minh, Cốc Vũ |
| Summer | Lập Hạ, Tiểu Mãn, Mang Chủng, Hạ Chí, Tiểu Thử, Đại Thử |
| Autumn | Lập Thu, Xử Thử, Bạch Lộ, Thu Phân, Hàn Lộ, Sương Giáng |
| Winter | Lập Đông, Tiểu Tuyết, Đại Tuyết, Đông Chí, Tiểu Hàn, Đại Hàn |

**[DESIGN]** This is a deterministic presentation join: `current solar term → seasonal bucket → source-faithful four-season paraphrase`. It is not a claim that *Suwen* prescribes a unique regimen for each term, and it is not local weather advice. UI copy should say “seasonal theme associated with the current solar-term season,” not “what your body needs today.”

## 3. Safety and wording contract

### 3.1 Institutional anchors

**[SOURCE]** WHO explicitly says that ICD-11's traditional-medicine chapter neither judges nor endorses the scientific validity of traditional-medicine practices or the efficacy of interventions. See the [WHO traditional-medicine ICD FAQ](https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/frequently-asked-questions/traditional-medicine).

**[SOURCE]** WHO publishes separate training benchmarks for acupuncture practitioners, intended to support evaluation and accreditation of service providers. See [WHO benchmarks for the training of acupuncture](https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789240017962).

**[SOURCE]** The U.S. National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health states that improperly delivered acupuncture can cause serious adverse effects, including infection, punctured organs, and central-nervous-system injury. See [NCCIH, Acupuncture: Effectiveness and Safety](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/acupuncture-effectiveness-and-safety).

**[SOURCE]** The FDA's January 2026 general-wellness guidance distinguishes software that encourages a healthy lifestyle from software connected to diagnosis, cure, mitigation, prevention, or treatment of disease. This is a U.S. regulatory boundary, not a determination of Vietnamese law, but it supplies a precise vocabulary distinction. See [FDA, General Wellness: Policy for Low Risk Devices](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices).

### 3.2 Required product wording

**[DESIGN]** Every output should carry a stable disclaimer, available to all serializers and not left to UI authors:

> Thông tin văn hóa–lịch sử về quan niệm dưỡng sinh truyền thống; không phải tư vấn y khoa, chẩn đoán, phòng ngừa hay điều trị. Không dùng để trì hoãn hoặc thay thế chăm sóc từ nhân viên y tế có chuyên môn.

Suggested English equivalent:

> Historical and cultural information about a traditional wellness system; not medical advice, diagnosis, prevention, or treatment. Do not use it to delay or replace care from a qualified health professional.

**[DESIGN]** Preferred phrasing:

- “Theo bảng nạp địa chi trong [source]...”
- “Kinh này được **gắn với** giờ Dần trong truyền thống...”
- “Văn bản cổ mô tả / khuyên...”
- “Chủ đề theo mùa...”
- “Historical association,” “classical-text description,” and “cultural routine.”

**[DESIGN]** Forbidden phrasing and fields:

- “Cơ quan đang hoạt động mạnh nhất,” “giờ thải độc,” “khí huyết đạt đỉnh,” or “best time to treat.”
- “Prevents,” “treats,” “cures,” “diagnoses,” “reduces risk,” “balances hormones,” or disease/symptom-specific verbs.
- Clinical `indication`, `contraindication`, `diagnosis`, `treatment`, `dose`, `needle_depth`, `point_to_press`, or `efficacy` fields.
- Instructions to stop, start, replace, or reschedule medicine or professional care.
- Acupuncture point names in Tier 0, even if framed as “for information,” because the product would be transforming a point-opening method into actionable procedure guidance.

## 4. Provenance and `source_id`

The expansion framework's proposed IDs (`ty-ngo-luu-chu`, `tiet-khi-duong-sinh`) are too broad for the narrowed, source-faithful Tier-0 scope.

### Recommended primitive sources

| Constant | `source_id` | Scope | Exact citation metadata |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SOURCE_SHI_ER_JING_NA_DI_ZHI` | `shi-er-jing-na-di-zhi` | Fixed branch → channel historical association only | Work, volume/chapter, section `十二經納地支歌`, edition/facsimile URI, transcription URI, access date |
| `SOURCE_HUANGDI_NEIJING_SUWEN` | `huangdi-neijing-suwen` | Four-season source paraphrases only | Chapter `四氣調神大論篇第二`, base edition, facsimile URI, passage key (`spring`, `summer`, `autumn`, `winter`) |
| Existing solar-term source | retain the existing calendar source already attached by Amlich | Astronomical current-term computation only | Existing algorithm/method/version; do not retag as *Suwen* |

**[DESIGN]** Reserve `ty-ngo-luu-chu` for a future, separately reviewed implementation of the full timed point-opening tradition. Do not emit that ID from the fixed association lookup.

**[DESIGN]** Do not use `tiet-khi-duong-sinh` as a primitive source ID. It collapses two operations that have different provenance: astronomical solar-term resolution and a *Suwen* four-season passage. If a composed result is exported through reasoning, emit the primitive solar-term and *Suwen* envelopes separately, plus one composite envelope such as `rule.composite.seasonal_wellness`.

### Minimum evidence payload

Each returned fact should make the following auditable:

```text
source_id
method_id + method_version
work_title
volume_or_chapter
passage_or_table_key
edition_or_facsimile_uri
transcription_uri
translation_kind = "project_paraphrase" | "none"
reviewer
review_status
time_basis (hour facts only)
safety_class = "historical_cultural_non_clinical"
known_divergence_ids[]
```

**[DESIGN]** Keep the Chinese channel identity (`手太陰肺`, etc.), Vietnamese label, and English gloss as separate fields. `心包` and `三焦` must not be silently converted into modern anatomical or biomedical equivalents.

## 5. Minimum golden and contract cases

The framework requires at least ten cases per pillar. This scope should exceed that minimum because both tables are finite.

### Branch–channel lookup

1. **12 table goldens:** one assertion for every Earthly Branch and exact channel identity in the table above.
2. **4 civil-time boundaries:** 22:59 → Hợi/Tam tiêu; 23:00 → Tý/Đởm; 00:59 → Tý/Đởm; 01:00 → Sửu/Can. These reuse, rather than redefine, the existing hour-pillar convention.
3. **12 round trips:** branch index, Vietnamese branch, Chinese branch, canonical channel ID, Vietnamese display, and Chinese channel string serialize and deserialize unchanged.
4. **Provenance contract:** every result uses only `shi-er-jing-na-di-zhi`; none uses `ty-ngo-luu-chu`.
5. **Divergence contract:** every result references the fixed-cycle historical-contestation `KnownDivergence`.

### Seasonal lookup

1. **24 table goldens:** every solar term maps to exactly one of four seasonal profiles, six terms per profile.
2. **8 edge goldens:** the term immediately before and at each of Lập Xuân, Lập Hạ, Lập Thu, and Lập Đông selects the expected old/new seasonal profile.
3. **4 corpus goldens:** one exact approved Vietnamese paraphrase per season, reviewed against the Chinese passage and facsimile.
4. **Provenance contract:** a composed result retains distinct solar-term and `huangdi-neijing-suwen` primitive evidence plus one composite envelope; it never claims *Suwen* computed the term.

### Safety and schema

1. Tier 0 succeeds without birth data, sex/gender, symptoms, location, or health history.
2. Public DTOs contain no acupuncture point, treatment, diagnosis, efficacy, food/herb, dose, or disease fields.
3. All localized outputs carry the disclaimer or a stable disclaimer ID that clients are contractually required to render.
4. A vocabulary guard rejects the prohibited clinical claims listed in §3.2 in the authored corpus.
5. Snapshot tests show neutral association wording, never “active/peak/best treatment time.”

## 6. Known divergences and open review ledger

| ID | Divergence | Current decision |
|---|---|---|
| `LH-DIV-01` | Full `子午流注` is a day/hour stem-branch point-opening method; common “organ clock” presentations reduce it to a fixed two-hour cycle. | Rename/scope the Tier-0 surface as `十二經納地支`; defer full method. |
| `LH-DIV-02` | Later classical authors preserve but explicitly criticize the fixed one-channel-per-double-hour allocation. | Surface as historical association with a mandatory divergence marker; no physiology claim. |
| `LH-DIV-03` | Classical timekeeping does not define modern civil-zone, DST, or longitude-correction behavior. | Reuse Amlich local civil hour branches and disclose `time_basis`; do not claim classical exactness. |
| `LH-DIV-04` | *Suwen* supplies four seasonal profiles, while the desired UI key is one of 24 solar terms. | Treat term → season as a transparent Amlich composition, not a direct source claim. |
| `LH-DIV-05` | Solar-term phenology was formed around the Yellow River; weather seasons differ by geography, including within China. | Do not turn the profile into local weather or exposure advice. |
| `LH-DIV-06` | Classical `臟腑` labels do not map one-to-one to modern anatomy/physiology; `心包` and `三焦` are especially unsafe to biomedicalize. | Preserve traditional names and use “channel” rather than “organ function.” |
| `LH-DIV-07` | The classical seasonal chapter includes claims about organ injury and later disease. | Do not surface those claims in Tier 0; retain only in source-audit notes. |

### Review gates before implementation

1. A qualified classical-Chinese reviewer verifies the twelve-channel verse against a chosen facsimile and signs all 12 records.
2. A qualified reviewer verifies the four Vietnamese paraphrases against `四氣調神大論`; no translation should be copied from a modern copyrighted edition.
3. Product/legal review approves the disclaimer and confirms the intended distribution jurisdictions. The FDA wording above is only a useful boundary model, not Vietnamese legal advice.
4. A health-safety reviewer confirms that no field can be interpreted as point selection or self-treatment guidance.

Until those reviews are complete, use the repo's explicit `ExternalReviewPending(...)` convention rather than marking records “primary” solely because an online transcription exists.

## 7. Proposed milestone boundary

**[DESIGN]** A source-faithful name would be **“Lunar Rhythms & Seasonal Wellness (Tier 0)”** or **“Thập nhị kinh nạp địa chi & Tứ khí điều thần.”** Avoid “Lunar Health” as the primary user-facing label if it implies clinical capability, and avoid “Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú” unless the full point-opening system is actually implemented under a separately approved clinical-safety scope.

Proposed deliverables:

1. Schema and ADR locking historical association, seasonal-profile, safety, and divergence fields.
2. Two primitive `source_id` registrations and source-literal guards.
3. A 12-record branch–channel corpus and a 4-record seasonal paraphrase corpus, both externally reviewable.
4. Pure Tier-0 lookup APIs and additive snapshot fields.
5. Separate primitive provenance plus a composite seasonal-wellness envelope.
6. The finite golden/contract suite in §5.

Explicit non-goals are every item in “Must be deferred” above.
