納甲法 per *Zhenjiu Dacheng* vol. 5) for an anonymous user selecting a local
date and time: the day-stem/hour-branch driven open channel, five-shu/original
point, and open/closed (閉穴) state, each row cited to the classical verse and
tables — procedural citation only, never treatment instruction, and separate

## Integration verification

1. **Additive compatibility** — `DaySnapshot.point_opening` is `Option`,
   serde-defaulted and absent from JSON when un-enriched
   (`ordinary_snapshots_stay_byte_compatible_until_point_opening_is_enriched`,
   `ordinary_snapshot_leaves_point_opening_absent_everywhere`,
   `ordinary_payload_round_trips_without_the_field`); enrichment adds exactly
   that key and leaves every other field — including v1.10
   `traditional_wellness` — untouched; populated open/closed snapshots
   round-trip byte-equal. `DayInfoDto` grows exactly one key; the desktop
   `ClassicalSurfaceDto` grows `point_opening` then
   `point_opening_citation_lines` additively; `PersonalDayChartDto` carries
   neither (`personal_day_chart_dto_does_not_carry_point_opening`).
2. **Assessment isolation** — point-opening never couples into Day Assessment,
   Hour Ranking, Direction Assessment, or the v1.10 Traditional Wellness
   Context: assessment surfaces stay byte-identical with and without
   enrichment (bead `.2.2.6`), and the contract guard locks the separation.
   Late-night (23:00+) enrichment discloses the frozen day transition rather
   than silently switching tables (`late_night_enrichment_discloses_the_frozen_day_transition`).
3. **Cross-surface parity** — core, API helper, Tauri command, TUI widget, and
   desktop inspector render the identical citation wording, byte-locked to the
   committed goldens on both the open and closed fixture; the TS fixture
   mirror is machine-verified against the same `.txt` goldens; the semantic
   graph projection is validated by the 9-test integration suite with locked
   concept-label round-trip.
4. **Review-state propagation** — every record and every surfaced context
   carries the union pending-review state, disclaimer v2, safety class
   `historical_procedural_citation`, time basis `local_civil_hour_branch`, and
   applicable `TNLC-DIV-*` divergences; `PENDING_CLASSICAL_REVIEW` stays
   visible in every citation until Gate 1 signs.
