# Requirements: Amlich v1.11 — Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú Point-Opening Context (Tier 1)

**Defined:** 2026-08-21
**Status:** Delivery beads published
**Research:** [`../research/TNLC_POINT_OPENING_RESEARCH.md`](../research/TNLC_POINT_OPENING_RESEARCH.md)
**Decisions:** [`../../docs/adr/0003-separate-branch-channel-association-from-ty-ngo-luu-chu.md`](../../docs/adr/0003-separate-branch-channel-association-from-ty-ngo-luu-chu.md), [`../../docs/adr/0004-implement-full-ty-ngo-luu-chu-under-procedural-citation-scope.md`](../../docs/adr/0004-implement-full-ty-ngo-luu-chu-under-procedural-citation-scope.md)

## Milestone goal

Give an anonymous user the classical Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú (子午流注, Xu-style 納甲法)
result for a selected local date and time: the historically cited open channel,
five-shu/original point, or explicit closed-hour state, resolved from the day
stem and hour branch and cited to *Zhenjiu Dacheng* vol. 5. The result is
procedural citation, never treatment instruction, and remains separate from Day
Assessment, Hour Ranking, Direction Assessment, and the v1.10 Traditional
Wellness Context.

## Product boundary

- Full point-opening computation, default consumer surface (owner decisions
  2026-08-21, ADR 0004), conditioned on disclaimer v2 and four human review
  gates.
- Citation framing only: no technique, depth, manipulation, indication,
  contraindication, efficacy, or action-recommendation content.
- The 徐氏 tables as printed in *Zhenjiu Dacheng* are canonical; closed slots
  stay closed; later filling schools are divergences, not merges.
- Tier 1 remains anonymous: no birth profile, sex/gender, symptoms, location,
  or health history.

## Requirements

### BOUND — Scope and safety boundary

- [ ] **BOUND-01** — A canonical Point-Opening Context resolves from local
  civil date/time and the existing day-pillar facts alone. Closed (閉穴) slots
  and unresolvable table rows serialize explicit unavailable states, never a
  fallback point or neutral wording.
- [ ] **BOUND-02** — Every serialized result carries the bilingual
  cultural-information disclaimer v2 and safety classification
  `historical_procedural_citation`. Public schemas contain no clinical,
  technique, depth, indication, contraindication, efficacy, or
  action-recommendation fields.

### SOURCE — Provenance and review

- [ ] **SOURCE-01** — Primitive method evidence uses only `ty-ngo-luu-chu`
  (first emission, satisfying ADR 0003's reservation), with work, volume,
  verse/table keys, facsimile/transcription references, and
  `local_civil_hour_branch` time basis. Pillar and calendar facts retain their
  existing engine sources; the Tier-0 `shi-er-jing-na-di-zhi` corpus is never
  cited for point rows.
- [ ] **SOURCE-02** — Every corpus record carries reviewer state, safety
  class, and applicable `TNLC-DIV-*` divergence IDs. All records remain
  `ExternalReviewPending` until their named human review gates sign; review
  dates cannot be silently deferred.

### TABLE — Frozen classical tables

- [ ] **TABLE-01** — The complete 10-stem × 12-hour open-point table set —
  including closed slots, 氣納三焦/血歸包絡 rows, and the cross-day spillover
  convention — is frozen from a chosen facsimile before engine freeze, and the
  engine resolves every query strictly from the frozen corpus. A selected
  datetime yields exactly one row: open (channel, point class, point identity)
  or closed, with no interpolation.

### NAME — Point nomenclature

- [ ] **NAME-01** — Every point identity is a triple: the classical Chinese
  穴名 exactly as printed, the Gate-2-signed Vietnamese huyệt danh, and a
  standard alphanumeric code as lookup gloss. Codes and names are never
  annotated with function, effect, or biomedical claims.

### EXPLAIN — Graph and consumer surfaces

- [ ] **EXPLAIN-01** — Core, API, terminal, and desktop expose the same
  point-opening citation, provenance, disclaimer v2, time basis, review state,
  and divergences, as an additive DTO field separate from v1.10's
  `traditional_wellness`. The semantic graph represents rows with citation
  semantics (e.g. `ClassicallyCitedOpenAt`); it does not assert physiological
  flow, organ performance, or treatment suitability.

### VERIFY — Finite and cross-surface gates

- [ ] **VERIFY-01** — Tests pin all 120 day-stem × hour-branch slots,
  cross-day spillover boundaries, civil-time hour boundaries, nomenclature
  round-trips, provenance separation from v1.10, closed-slot unavailability,
  additive serialization compatibility, cross-surface parity, and an extended
  prohibited-language guard (no châm/bấm/cứu/best-time/treat instruction
  lexicon). Full Rust, JavaScript/Svelte, and production build gates pass.

## Human review gates

The milestone cannot ship until:

1. A qualified classical-Chinese reviewer signs the chosen facsimile and every
   frozen table row (all 120 slots, spillover, closed rows).
2. A qualified reviewer signs the Vietnamese huyệt danh set and code glosses.
3. A health-safety reviewer confirms no schema, wording, or surface behavior
   can be read as point-selection instruction or self-treatment encouragement,
   including the default-surface exposure decision.
4. Product/legal review approves disclaimer v2 and the intended distribution
   jurisdictions.

## Explicitly out of scope

- 靈龜八法 (Linggui Bafa) and 飛騰八法 (Feiteng Bafa) eight-method points;
  養子時刻注穴法 (hour-stem variant).
- Needling, moxibustion, acupressure technique, depth, 補瀉 as instruction,
  contraindication, or first-aid guidance.
- Efficacy, prevention, treatment, or diagnosis claims; Tier-2 Bazi
  personalization.
- Any coupling into Day Assessment, Hour Ranking, Direction Assessment, or the
  v1.10 Traditional Wellness Context.

## Release gates

- All eight requirements have implementation evidence.
- All four human review gates are signed or the affected output remains
  unavailable; review dates cannot be silently deferred.
- The v1.11 milestone audit records requirements, integration, compatibility,
  safety, and full quality-gate evidence.
- Repository and beads state are committed, synchronized, and pushed.
