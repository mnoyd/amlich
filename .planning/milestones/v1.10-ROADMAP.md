## Delivery sequence

### Track 1 — Selected-hour branch-channel association

Deliver the complete anonymous path from a local civil time to one historical
Twelve-Branch Channel Association through core, API, terminal, desktop,
provenance, review state, disclaimer, divergence notice, and finite golden
tests. This track includes classical-text and safety review and can begin
without the seasonal track.

### Track 2 — Selected-date seasonal cultivation context

Replace the existing unsourced/clinical-sounding Tiết khí health copy with four
reviewable *Suwen* seasonal paraphrases, joined transparently to all 24 solar
terms through the existing calendar engine. Deliver the complete path through
core, API, terminal, desktop, provenance, review state, disclaimer, transition
goldens, and copy-safety guards. This track can proceed in parallel with Track
1.

### Track 3 — Unified explanation and semantic graph

After both primitive paths exist, expose one cross-surface Traditional Wellness
Context explanation. Preserve the two primitive source envelopes plus the
seasonal composite envelope, render review/divergence/time-basis details, and
represent the channel relation as an association rather than physiological
flow.

### Track 4 — Audit and release

Verify all eight requirements, four human review gates, additive compatibility,
finite goldens, cross-surface parity, lexical/schema safety, and full workspace
quality gates. Publish the milestone audit and reconcile planning state only
after the evidence passes.

## Dependencies

- Tracks 1 and 2 can start independently.
- Track 3 depends on Tracks 1 and 2.
- Track 4 depends on Track 3 and all human review gates.

## Exit criteria

- The user can inspect a selected hour and selected date without supplying any
  personal or medical data.
- Every claim is source-attributed, reviewable, and bounded as historical
  cultural information.
- No output claims clinical meaning or affects another assessment/ranking.
- All requirements and release gates pass and the milestone audit is published.
