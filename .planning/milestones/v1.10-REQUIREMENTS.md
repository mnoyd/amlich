## Milestone goal

Give an anonymous user a source-attributed, non-clinical Traditional Wellness
Context for a selected local date and time: one historical Twelve-Branch
Channel Association and one four-season cultivation profile selected through
the active solar term. The result must preserve historical disagreement,
explain its time basis and composition, and remain separate from Day
Assessment, Hour Ranking, Direction Assessment, and medical guidance.

## Product boundary

- Tier 0 only: no birth profile, sex/gender, symptoms, location, health
  history, or Bazi personalization.
- Historical and cultural information only. No diagnosis, prevention,
  treatment, efficacy, acupuncture point, food/herb/supplement, dose, or
  disease claims.
- The fixed branch-to-channel table is **Thập nhị kinh nạp địa chi**
  (`十二經納地支`), not full Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú (`子午流注`).
- The seasonal corpus contains four source-grounded profiles. Mapping the 24
  solar terms into those four seasons is an explicit Amlich composition, not
  24 classical regimens.
- Traditional Wellness Context is informational; it never moves, vetoes, or
  changes another assessment or ranking.

## Requirements

### BOUND — Scope and safety boundary

- [ ] **BOUND-01** — A canonical Traditional Wellness contract succeeds from
  date, local civil time, and existing calendar facts alone; it is unavailable
  only when those inputs cannot resolve, never because personal or medical data
  is absent.
- [ ] **BOUND-02** — Every serialized result carries the stable Vietnamese and
  English cultural-information disclaimer and a safety classification. Public
  schemas contain no clinical, procedure, point-selection, prescription, or
  efficacy fields.

### SOURCE — Provenance and review

- [ ] **SOURCE-01** — Primitive evidence uses only
  `shi-er-jing-na-di-zhi` for branch-channel associations and
  `huangdi-neijing-suwen` for the four seasonal paraphrases. Astronomical
  solar-term evidence retains its existing source; the seasonal join adds
  `rule.composite.seasonal_wellness`. `ty-ngo-luu-chu` is reserved and never
  emitted in v1.10.
- [ ] **SOURCE-02** — Every corpus record carries work/chapter or table key,
  facsimile/transcription references, project-paraphrase status, reviewer
  state, safety class, and applicable `KnownDivergence` IDs. The 12 association
  records, four seasonal paraphrases, disclaimer, and safety schema remain
  `ExternalReviewPending` until their named human review gates are signed.

### ASSOCIATION — Selected-hour context

- [ ] **ASSOC-01** — A user selecting any local civil time receives exactly
  one of the 12 source-pinned Earthly Branch-to-Traditional Channel
  associations, including stable branch/channel identity, bilingual display,
  Chinese channel identity, two-hour window, `local_civil_hour_branch` time
  basis, neutral “associated with” wording, and the historical-contestation
  divergence marker.

### SEASON — Selected-date context

- [ ] **SEASON-01** — A selected date resolves through the existing solar-term
  engine to exactly one spring/summer/autumn/winter profile, with all 24 terms
  covered six per season and transitions anchored at Lập Xuân, Lập Hạ, Lập
  Thu, and Lập Đông. The output clearly distinguishes source paraphrase from
  Amlich's term-to-season composition and never presents local weather or a
  term-specific prescription.

### EXPLAIN — Graph and consumer surfaces

- [ ] **EXPLAIN-01** — Core, API, terminal, and desktop consumers expose the
  same association, seasonal profile, primitive/composite provenance,
  disclaimer, time basis, review state, and divergences. The semantic graph
  uses `TraditionalChannel` plus association semantics such as
  `AssociatedWithHourBranch`; it does not assert physiological flow.

### VERIFY — Finite and cross-surface gates

- [ ] **VERIFY-01** — Tests pin all 12 association rows, four civil-time
  boundaries (22:59, 23:00, 00:59, 01:00), all 24 term-to-season mappings,
  eight seasonal transition edges, all four reviewed paraphrases, provenance
  separation, additive serialization compatibility, Tier-0 availability,
  cross-surface parity, and a lexical/schema guard against prohibited clinical
  language. Full Rust, JavaScript/Svelte, and production build gates pass.

## Human review gates

The milestone cannot ship until:

1. A qualified classical-Chinese reviewer signs the chosen facsimile and all
   12 branch-channel records.
2. A qualified reviewer signs the four Vietnamese project paraphrases against
   `四氣調神大論`.
3. Product/legal review approves the bilingual disclaimer for intended
   distribution jurisdictions.
4. A health-safety reviewer confirms no schema or copy can be read as point
   selection or self-treatment guidance.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Full Tý Ngọ Lưu Chú / `納甲法`, timed point opening, acupuncture points,
  needling, moxibustion, acupressure, or treatment-time output.
- Claims that a channel or organ is active, at peak, detoxing, weak, blocked,
  or diagnostically meaningful at a given hour.
- Disease/symptom/risk/prevention/treatment claims and food, herb, supplement,
  fasting, or quantified sleep/exercise prescriptions.
- Twenty-four unique solar-term regimens, local-weather advice, and Tier-2
  Bazi personalization.
- P5 Spatial Phi Tinh, P6 Tử Vi Đẩu Số, Hỗ Quả, and I Ching Bazi enrichment.

## Release gates

- All eight requirements have implementation evidence.
- All four human review gates are signed or the affected output remains
  unavailable; review dates cannot be silently deferred.
- The v1.10 milestone audit records requirements, integration, compatibility,
  safety, and full quality-gate evidence.
- Repository and beads state are committed, synchronized, and pushed.
