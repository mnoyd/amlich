## Delivery sequence

### Track 1 — Classical table research, freeze, and reviewer pack

Bead: `amlich-xlag.2.1`. Transcribe and freeze the complete Xu-style 納甲法
table set from a chosen *Zhenjiu Dacheng* vol. 5 facsimile: all 10 day-stem ×
12 hour rows, closed (閉穴) slots, 氣納三焦/血歸包絡 rows, cross-day spillover,
and point 穴名 strings exactly as printed. Publish the corpus
`ExternalReviewPending` with per-row evidence links and the REVIEWER-PACK for
Gates 1–4. The research and transcription work is agent-ready now; the freeze
records remain unsigned until Gate 1.

### Track 2 — Core engine, policy contract, and goldens

Bead: `amlich-xlag.2.2`. After the table freeze: `TY_NGO_LUU_CHU_POLICY_V1`
contract, first emission of the reserved `ty-ngo-luu-chu` source, pure
resolver from day stem + hour branch to one frozen row (open channel, point
class, point identity triple, or explicit closed state), additive
`DaySnapshot` field, semantic-graph citation semantics, nomenclature
round-trips, and the finite golden suite (120 slots, spillover, boundaries,
provenance separation, prohibited-language extension).

### Track 3 — Unified projection across surfaces

Bead: `amlich-xlag.2.3`. Project the identical point-opening citation through
API, terminal, and desktop on the default surface, with disclaimer v2, review
state, time basis, and divergence details; cross-surface parity and additive
compatibility tests.

### Track 4 — Audit and release

Bead: `amlich-xlag.2.4`. Verify all eight requirements, four human review
gates, additive compatibility, finite goldens, cross-surface parity,
lexical/schema safety, and full workspace quality gates. Publish the milestone
audit and reconcile planning state only after the evidence passes.

### Human review gates

Beads: `amlich-xlag.2.5` (classical-Chinese table sign-off), `.2.6`
(Vietnamese point-nomenclature sign-off), `.2.7` (health-safety sign-off
including default-surface exposure), `.2.8` (product/legal sign-off on
disclaimer v2). Due 2026-12-31. Coordinator playbook follows the v1.10
REVIEWER-COORDINATION pattern.

## Dependencies

- Track 1 can start immediately (research/transcription) but its freeze
  records await Gate 1.
- Track 2 depends on Track 1's frozen corpus.
- Track 3 depends on Track 2.
- Track 4 depends on Track 3 and all four gates.
- v1.10's pending gates (`amlich-l2zc.5`–`.8`) are independent but share the
  reviewer-coordination machinery; signing experience there should inform this
  milestone's packs.

## Exit criteria

- The user can inspect the classical point-opening citation for any selected
  local datetime without supplying personal or medical data.
- Every row is facsimile-frozen, source-attributed, reviewable, and bounded as
  historical procedural citation; closed slots stay closed.
- No output instructs, encourages, or schedules any intervention, and nothing
  affects another assessment or ranking.
- All requirements and release gates pass and the milestone audit is
  published.
