<feature>
  <name>INT-06 2026 E2E calendar smoke</name>
  <files>crates/amlich-core/tests/integration_2026_smoke.rs</files>
  <behavior>
    - A `Vec<(i32,i32,i32)>` of ≥30 DISTINCT (day,month,year) dates is assembled covering: Tết 2026 (2026-02-17); Sóc (lunar day 1) ×12 months of 2026; Vọng (lunar day 15) ×12 months of 2026; the Vận 8→9 boundary straddle (2024-02-03 and 2024-02-05); the 2026 leap month 6 (3 dates in solar late-Jul/Aug 2026); all 24 Tiết Khí boundaries of 2026 via `terms_for_year(2026)`.
    - For EACH date: `calculate_day_snapshot` returns without panic; `find_van_khan_for_snapshot(&snap)` returns (may be empty); `compute_combined_overlay(year, lunar-or-solar month as u8, &scanner).palace_overlays.len() == 9`; `compute_palace_aspects(...).len() == 9`.
    - The Vận boundary assertions hold: `compute_period(jd_from_date(3,2,2024), &scanner).van == 8` and `compute_period(jd_from_date(5,2,2024), &scanner).van == 9`.
    - The Tết assertion holds: snapshot for 2026-02-17 has `context.lunar.day == 1 && context.lunar.month == 1`.
  </behavior>
  <implementation>
    Build the date set programmatically:
    - Tết: hardcode (17,2,2026).
    - Sóc/Vọng ×12: iterate a day-cursor across 2026 calling `calculate_day_snapshot(d,m,2026)`; collect the first solar date in each lunar month whose `lunar.day == 1` (Sóc) and `== 15` (Vọng). Simpler robust approach: scan solar 2026-01-01 .. 2026-12-31 via `jd_from_date`/`jd_to_date`, convert each, and for each (lunar_month) record the first date hitting day==1 and day==15. Dedup. This yields ~12 Sóc + ~12 Vọng.
    - Vận straddle: (3,2,2024) and (5,2,2024) — add to the date list AND assert their `compute_period(...).van`.
    - Leap month 6 2026: pick 3 solar dates whose converted lunar is month 6 with `is_leap == true` (scan and filter `lunar.month == 6 && lunar.is_leap`); take the first 3.
    - 24 Tiết Khí: `TietKhiScanner::new().terms_for_year(2026)` → map `jd_to_date(t.jd)` (remember order is (d,m,y)).
    - Collect all into a deduped Vec; `assert!(dates.len() >= 30)`.
    - Loop: for each (d,m,y) call the four pillar APIs and assert non-panic + array lengths == 9. For the month argument to `compute_combined_overlay`/`compute_palace_aspects`, pass the snapshot's `context.lunar.month as u8` (the monthly Phi Tinh layer is lunar/solar-term anchored; use the lunar month consistent with how 15-01 populated `flying_stars`). Clamp/guard month into 1..=12 if needed.

    Use `#[test] fn e2e_2026_smoke_all_categories()` as the main entry; optionally split the Vận-boundary and Tết assertions into their own small `#[test]` fns for clear failure localization. External-consumer imports only.
  </implementation>
</feature>
