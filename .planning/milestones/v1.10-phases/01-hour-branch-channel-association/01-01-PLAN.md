<objective>
Register the `shi-er-jing-na-di-zhi` source ID, ship the 12-row branch-channel corpus and its bilingual disclaimer as `ExternalReviewPending`, expose `resolve_hour_branch_association` and `enrich_day_snapshot_with_branch_channel_association` in `amlich_core::traditional_wellness`, and lock the boundary / provenance / divergence / safety contracts with golden tests and a prohibited-language guard.

Purpose: ASSOC-01 + SOURCE-01 + SOURCE-02 + BOUND-01 + BOUND-02 are the five requirements owned by bead `amlich-l2zc.1`. They are *informational and additive* — they never change Day Assessment, Hour Ranking, Direction Assessment, or any other surface. Plan 01-01 deliberately stops short of (a) writing the DaySnapshot additive field and (b) wiring the API/TUI/desktop surfaces; those belong to plan 01-02 (the `.1`-`.3` bridge once `.2`'s seasonal shape is known). Plan 01-02 will also produce the cross-surface parity test for `EXPLAIN-01`.

Output: A `traditional_wellness` module that compiles, loads, and serializes the 12-row corpus; an enrich helper that any caller can attach to an existing `DaySnapshot`; a reviewer-ready corpus JSON with the same citation metadata the reviewer pack exposes; and the four human-review-gate sign-off slots wired into every record.

Out of scope (carried to other plans): API DTO + `convert.rs` mirror, TUI widget, desktop TS mirror, `EXPLAIN-01` semantic-graph nodes/edges, `KnownDivergence` registry cross-walk, and the lexical guard over the future DTO schemas (DTOs don't exist yet in plan 01-01).
</objective>
