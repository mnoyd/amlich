- `crates/amlich-core/src/traditional_wellness/branch_channel.rs` — review-state union helper: `Signed` wins; the first `Pending` otherwise.

- `crates/amlich-core/src/semantic_graph/views/helpers.rs::cluster_for_node_id` — `day-core` cluster (matches `Hexagram` / `Ritual`).
- `crates/amlich-core/src/semantic_graph/ids.rs` — add `SemanticId::traditional_channel(source, channel_zh)`, `SemanticId::seasonal_profile(source, season)`, `SemanticId::traditional_wellness_day_root(date, tz)` (reserved for future per-day roots).
- `crates/amlich-core/src/semantic_graph/builders/day_snapshot.rs::DaySnapshotGraphBuilder` — new `add_traditional_wellness_facts(snapshot)` method that early-returns when `snapshot.traditional_wellness.is_none()`. When populated:
  - One `TraditionalChannel` node per resolved branch-channel row, with `AssociatedWithHourBranch` edge to the canonical day root (the same root the v1.7–v1.9 builders emit; no parallel per-day root).

**Acceptance:**

- Enriched snapshots: exactly 1 of each per populated side; total 4 evidence envelopes (1 + 3) on the context.
- `TraditionalChannel` provenance carries `shi-er-jing-na-di-zhi`; `SeasonalProfile` provenance carries all three source ids.
- Both new edge concepts have `to_node_id == day_root` (the canonical day:YYYY-MM-DD:TZ root).
